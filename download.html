A free tool for EverQuest players

Chart Your Own

Chart your own travels. The atlas reads the log EverQuest already writes and draws every zone you've walked, in the same inked style as ours. It doesn't touch the game, and nothing leaves your computer unless you choose to publish.

Download

Download the atlas (.zip) Windows · needs Python 3.9 or newer · no other installs

The full instructions are in README.md inside the zip. Here's the short version.

Getting started

  1. Install Python

    From python.org. On the first screen of the installer, tick Add python.exe to PATH.

  2. Have Brewall's maps

    The charts are drawn over Brewall's maps, so they need to be in your EverQuest maps\Brewall folder. Most players have them already.

  3. Unzip, and name your party

    Unzip the atlas anywhere. If you play more than one character together, open config.json in Notepad and list them:

    "team": ["Mychar", "Myboxone", "Myboxtwo"],

    Playing solo? Skip this. The atlas finds your EverQuest folder, server and character by itself.

  4. Log on, and /loc often

    In game, type /log on once per character. Then use /loc as you travel. It's the only position the log records, so the more often you use it, the finer your routes. Most people bind it to a key they press constantly.

  5. Run it

    Double-click atlas_live.bat before you play and leave the window open: the atlas updates itself every five minutes. Then open journey\index.html in your browser. In game, your route is the third layer in the map window, and appears after your next zone change.

Want your atlas on the web like this one? It can publish itself to a free GitHub Pages site while you play, showing other players only as "the raid". See Publishing your atlas in the README.

What you get

Zone charts

Your route, fading with age; kills clustered where you camped; named foes starred; deaths and levels marked; screenshots pinned where you took them.

An in-game layer

The same route on EverQuest's map window, as its own checkbox. Add your own pins for vendors, camps and quest givers.

The chronicle

An index of every zone, a day-by-day Sessions page with your journal notes, a Bestiary of named slain, and the Fallen.

Zone outlines come from Brewall's EverQuest maps, read from your own install; they are not included in the download. EverQuest is a trademark of Daybreak Game Company; this is a fan project with no connection to Daybreak.
